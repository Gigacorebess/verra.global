About Verra Global

An international trading business.

Verra Global F.Z.E. is a UAE-based international trading venture engaged in strategic materials, commodities, textiles and apparel.

Reach

The company aims to develop cross-border commercial relationships connecting qualified suppliers, manufacturers and buyers across international markets.

Focus

Operating through both principal trading and trade facilitation models, Verra focuses on supplier qualification, buyer development, commercial negotiations, product verification and coordinated transaction execution.

Approach

With a relationship-driven approach and an emphasis on transparency, verification and structured execution, Verra seeks to develop sustainable international trade partnerships across its business verticals.

How Verra trades

Two trading models.

Each transaction is set up under the model that suits it, and the model is agreed with the counterparties before commitments are made.

Model A

Principal trading

Verra buys products directly from suppliers and resells them to buyers, managing the commercial transaction and the associated supply arrangements.

Model B

Trade facilitation

Verra facilitates transactions between qualified buyers and sellers, supporting sourcing, introductions, negotiations and transaction coordination against an agreed commercial fee or commission.
