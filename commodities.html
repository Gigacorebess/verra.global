Vertical 1 — Strategic materials & commodities

Where verification is hardest.

Verra trades strategic materials and commodities, from rare earths and critical minerals to coal, metals, fertilizers and selected industrial products. Products without a label are backed by documented supply opportunities. Products marked "Sourced on enquiry" are sourced against specific buyer requirements. Neither means stock is held or continuously available.
