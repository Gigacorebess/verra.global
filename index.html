FAQ

Common questions.

What is VCMX?

VCMX is a managed transaction desk and operating system for critical-mineral and selected physical-commodity transactions.

Is VCMX a marketplace?

Not in the conventional sense. VCMX can draw on marketplace and trade-data information, but its main role is to move a specific mandate through discovery, verification and execution.

What is an Executable Offer?

It is a transaction option where the key components (material, counterparty, commercial terms, compliance, inspection, payment, logistics and delivery) have been organized and their evidence status made clear.

Does VCMX guarantee every seller or shipment?

No. Verification is evidence-based and specific to each transaction. Any unresolved condition remains subject to diligence, documentation and a definitive agreement.

Does AI make binding trades automatically?

No. AI and automation help with research, analysis, documentation and workflow. Authorized people control binding commercial, legal, compliance and payment decisions.

How does VCMX verify counterparties?

Checks can include corporate identity and authority, KYC/KYB, beneficial-ownership review, sanctions and compliance review, trade history and transaction-specific evidence.

How is material verified?

Depending on the transaction, evidence can include COAs, assays, lot and batch records, title or release authority, physical inspection, and independent sampling and testing.

Are buyer and seller identities public?

No. VCMX is built around controlled disclosure and private transaction environments. Sensitive counterparty relationships are not published by default.

How do I start?

Submit the commodity, specification, quantity, destination, timing and any origin or commercial constraints. The transaction desk will structure the requirement and identify the next diligence and sourcing steps.
