Privacy notice

How we handle your information.

Last updated: 2 November 2026. This notice explains what information VERRA GLOBAL F.Z.E collects through verrafze.com and how it is used.

01

Who we are

VERRA GLOBAL F.Z.E, a Free Zone Establishment in the Ajman Free Zone, United Arab Emirates, operates this website and the VCMX transaction desk. Contact: info@verrafze.com.

02

What we collect

Only what you enter in the requirement form: buy or sell side, commodity, specification, quantity, frequency, destination, delivery window, delivery basis, any constraints you describe, company name, your name, business email and, if you choose, a phone or WhatsApp number. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

03

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, understand your requirement, and carry out the diligence and compliance checks needed before any transaction. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

04

Service providers

The site is hosted by Vercel. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. These providers process data under their own privacy terms and may store it outside the UAE.

05

Sharing

We share your details with a potential counterparty, inspector, bank or logistics provider only when that is needed to progress your requirement, or where the law requires it. Your identity is not disclosed to counterparties without your agreement.

06

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to handle your requirement and any resulting transaction, and for record-keeping required by law. You can ask us to delete it at any time, subject to those legal obligations.

07

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the information you sent us, or withdraw your consent, by emailing info@verrafze.com. We handle requests in line with applicable data-protection law, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law.

08

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
