Vertical 2 — Textiles, fabrics & apparel

Textiles, fabrics and apparel.

Textiles and apparel are one of Verra's two principal business verticals. Verra sources and trades finished garments, fabrics and fashion-related products internationally, drawing on its founder's long experience in fashion manufacturing, sourcing and apparel retail.

What we trade

Product categories.

Specific products, quantities and specifications are agreed per requirement. Listing a category does not mean stock is held.

01

Finished garments

Menswear, womenswear, kidswear, workwear, uniforms and activewear.

02

Woven & knitted fabrics

Cotton, synthetic and blended wovens, denim and linen; jersey, interlock, rib, fleece and piqué knits.

03

Textile & fashion products

Home textiles, fashion accessories and other textile-related products.

04

International apparel sourcing

Sourcing and trading for brands, retailers and distributors across international markets.

05

Private-label partnerships

Garment and fabric development and manufacturing partnerships, where applicable.

06

Trading models

Principal trading or trade facilitation, agreed per transaction.

How Verra works in textiles

Structured sourcing, start to finish.

The same structured, transparent approach Verra applies to commodities, adapted to apparel and textile supply chains.

01

Requirement & costing

Product, construction, fabric, sizing, quantity, target price and delivery window are defined up front.

02

Supplier qualification

Manufacturers and mills are assessed for capability, capacity, compliance and quality systems before any order.

03

Sampling & approval

Fabric, fit and pre-production samples are reviewed and approved before bulk production.

04

Commercial negotiation

Pricing, payment terms, lead times and delivery basis are negotiated and documented.

05

Quality inspection

Inline and final inspections, including independent third-party checks where agreed.

06

Logistics & delivery

Shipping, documentation and delivery are coordinated through to the agreed destination.
